## Section-by-Section Academic Style Guide

### Introduction

**Purpose**: Establish research background, articulate the problem, and define study objectives.

```
❌ In today's modern society, there are a very wide variety of complicated problems occurring across many sectors, and numerous studies have been actively conducted to address these various issues over recent years.

✅ Accelerating digital transformation has exacerbated intergenerational skill divides across knowledge-intensive organizations. As of 2023, 73% of enterprise firms identify cross-generational workforce coordination as a primary operational challenge (Kerr et al., 2023).
```

### Literature Review

**Purpose**: Synthesize existing research and establish the theoretical framework.

```
❌ Smith (2020) argued in his study that A affects B. In addition, Jones (2021) also conducted a similar study and observed that there is some relationship between A and B. Furthermore, Lee (2022) confirmed this relationship as well.

✅ Prior literature on the relationship between variable A and variable B converges into three primary streams. First, Smith (2020) demonstrated direct causality in laboratory settings. Second, Jones (2021) identified moderator variable C as a critical boundary condition. Third, Lee (2022) qualitatively explored the micro-mechanisms governing this link.
```

### Methodology

**Purpose**: Describe research design, data collection protocols, and analytical techniques.

```
❌ In this study, a survey method was attempted to be used to collect data from participants, and the collected data were then analyzed through a statistical software package.

✅ Data collection proceeded via an online cross-sectional survey. The sampling frame comprised 320 undergraduate students recruited through stratified quota sampling across four metropolitan universities. All data were analyzed using SPSS 26.0 and Amos 28.0.
```

### Results

**Purpose**: Present empirical findings objectively without premature interpretation.

```
❌ As a result of the statistical analysis, Hypothesis 1 was found to be statistically significantly supported, which can be seen as a very meaningful and important finding.

✅ Multiple regression analysis supported Hypothesis 1: variable A exerted a significant positive effect on variable B (β = .342, p < .001). This effect remained robust after controlling for demographic covariates (Model 2: β = .298, p < .01).
```

### Discussion

**Purpose**: Interpret findings, establish theoretical contributions, and delineate boundary conditions.

```
❌ These research results are thought to have really interesting and significant implications because prior studies have not produced this kind of outcome before.

✅ These findings extend existing theory in two critical respects. First, empirical validation in non-Western organizational contexts confirms the cross-cultural generalizability of the baseline construct. Second, moderated mediation analyses delineate the structural boundary conditions under which the observed effect attenuates.
```

### Conclusion

**Purpose**: Summarize core contributions, disclose methodological limitations, and propose future research.

```
❌ This study has several limitations, and it is considered that future research should supplement these limitations and conduct further improved investigations.

✅ Two principal methodological limitations qualify these findings. First, the cross-sectional design restricts causal inference. Second, single-source self-reports introduce potential common method variance. Longitudinal multi-source designs are recommended to track these behavioral dynamics over time.
```

## Discipline-Specific Vocabulary Guidelines

### Social Sciences

| Phrases to Avoid | Recommended Scholarly Phrasing |
|------------------|--------------------------------|
| gives an effect to / impacts on | exerts an effect on, influences, drives |
| has a relationship with | correlates with, is associated with, covaries with |
| there is a difference | exhibits a significant disparity, diverges significantly |
| goes up / goes down | increases / decreases, scales positively / attenuates |
| is considered to be | indicates, demonstrates, suggests |

### Humanities

| Phrases to Avoid | Recommended Scholarly Phrasing |
|------------------|--------------------------------|
| is seen as | is interpreted as, functions as, signifies |
| shows | reveals, manifests, discloses, articulates |
| talks about | examines, interrogates, explicates, delineates |
| appears | emerges, materializes, crystallizes |
| thinks that | posits, contends, theorizes |

### Natural Sciences & Engineering

| Phrases to Avoid | Recommended Scholarly Phrasing |
|------------------|--------------------------------|
| we tried an experiment | we performed the experiment, we measured |
| results came out | results revealed, data yielded, was observed |
| good performance | enhanced throughput, superior efficiency, optimized fidelity |
| works well | operates within nominal parameters, satisfies design specifications |
| got bigger / smaller | amplified, expanded / diminished, contracted |

## Frequent Error Patterns and Corrections

### 1. Passive Voice Overuse & Agent Obscurity

```
❌ The investigation was carried out, and data collection was accomplished by the researchers.
✅ The researchers conducted the investigation and collected the survey data.

❌ Significant performance gains were achieved to be seen in the benchmark tests.
✅ The proposed algorithm outperformed baseline models by 14.2% across benchmark tests.
```

### 2. Missing or Vague Grammatical Subject

```
❌ In the analysis, a significant difference was observed.
✅ The analysis revealed a significant performance disparity between the two treatment cohorts.

❌ As examined in the theoretical background.
✅ As detailed in Section 2.3, the cognitive load framework accounts for this discrepancy.
```

### 3. Ambiguous Demonstratives and Referents

```
❌ These results show that this is critical for that process.
✅ These experimental results indicate that mediator variable A is essential for catalytic activation.

❌ It is consistent with previous research.
✅ This coefficient pattern aligns with the empirical findings of Smith (2020).
```

### 4. Excessive Hedging and Throat-Clearing

```
❌ It would seem to be possible that one might perhaps argue that...
✅ These findings suggest that...

❌ It is deemed to be considered as an indicator of...
✅ This parameter indicates...
```

## Sentence Transformation Exercises

### Original vs. Revised Comparison

**Original 1**:
> In this study, we attempted to examine the very complex relationships existing between a wide variety of different variables, through which we hoped to derive academically meaningful implications for the broader field.

**Revised 1**:
> This study examines the structural relationships among variables A, B, and C to derive theoretical and managerial implications.

---

**Original 2**:
> The survey questionnaire items were modified and adapted from measurement instruments whose validity and reliability had been verified in existing prior studies to fit the specific context of the present research.

**Revised 2**:
> Survey items were adapted from Smith's (2020) validated scale. While the original instrument exhibited internal consistency of α = .89, the adapted scale demonstrated α = .85 in our sample.

---

**Original 3**:
> As a result of the research, it was confirmed that there was a statistically significant positive effect relationship between the independent variable and the dependent variable, and this result was found to be consistent with the results of existing prior studies.

**Revised 3**:
> The independent variable positively influenced the dependent variable (β = .45, p < .001), corroborating the baseline findings of Jones (2021).
